## Direction contract

THESIS: SafeSpace should feel like a clear route through care, not a collection of disconnected tools; it refuses dark panels, generic dashboard chrome, and visual noise.

OWN-WORLD: A soft-green campus terrain and warm-white field sheets organize the app. Deep evergreen ink carries hierarchy, pale green marks active routes, and amber, red, and blue remain reserved for meaningful status states. Navigation, cards, forms, lists, maps, and charts share rounded 12–16px surfaces, quiet borders, and soft offset depth.

STORY: A student or counselor can see where they are, understand what needs attention, and take the next safe step without decoding the interface. The active route line appears in navigation, page headers, session states, and progress moments.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The shell opens with a calm green navigation rail, a warm-white topbar, a compact page title, and the page's primary task or current state above supporting content. The first action sits beside the title or directly beneath the key information, never below a wall of cards.

FORM: Assigned direction 4, orienteering map and legend; seed:320f4592782f40cb. Translate terrain into a restrained green-and-warm-white route grammar, keeping standard web navigation and controls rather than mimicking a physical map.
