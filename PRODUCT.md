# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and counselors use the application equally. Students seek private mental-health support, counseling sessions, resources, and self-care tools. Counselors manage session requests, conversations, resources, announcements, and student support workflows.

## Product Purpose

SafeSpace is the University of Santo Tomas - Legazpi Mental Health Support web application. It connects students with counselors and university mental-health resources through semi-anonymous counseling, scheduling, messaging, crisis support, self-care content, journaling, and university updates.

## Positioning

The product combines semi-anonymous student counseling with local university resource mapping, allowing students to seek support while retaining control over identity visibility.

## Operating Context

Students use the app to find help, book or join counseling sessions, message counselors, explore resources, record journal entries, and read OGT announcements. Counselors use role-specific dashboards to respond to requests, manage schedules, communicate with students, and maintain support content. Administrators register and manage university accounts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Role-based student, counselor, and administrator routes.
- Chat, voice calls, session booking, face-to-face scheduling, notifications, and session feedback.
- Resource mapping, self-care modules, diary entries, mood tracking, announcements, and suggestions.
- Crisis detection and safety workflows must remain available and clear.
- The redesign must preserve current routes, copy, logo/assets, and existing interactions.
- The existing React, Vite, TailwindCSS, DaisyUI, Leaflet, Recharts, Axios, Zustand, and Socket.IO stack remains in place.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: SafeSpace.
- Institution: University of Santo Tomas - Legazpi, Office of Guidance & Testing.
- The visual redesign uses soft green and warm white as its core palette.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing product documentation and feature history in `README.md`.
- Existing routes, components, assets, and token system in `frontend/src`.
- Existing UST-Legazpi logo asset reference in `frontend/src/pages/LoginPage.jsx`.

## Product Principles

- Make reaching support feel safe, calm, and non-threatening.
- Preserve student privacy and make identity choices understandable.
- Put the next useful action close to the information needed to choose it.
- Keep counselor and administrator workflows efficient without exposing unnecessary complexity to students.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The redesigned interface must meet WCAG AA expectations, including readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, accessible controls, and touch-friendly targets.
